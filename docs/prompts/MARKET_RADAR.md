# DUTRA OS — Market & Trend Radar

Purpose: continuously look beyond the current implementation and identify evidence-backed opportunities without turning hype into scope.

## Research domains

Scan only what is relevant to DUTRA OS and OG:

1. B2B sales execution
2. AI agents / agentic sales workflows
3. CRM and account intelligence
4. conversation intelligence / call analysis
5. proposal / presentation / digital sales-room UX
6. quoting, CPQ, ROI and sales enablement
7. fleet / trucking / logistics technology
8. mobile / PWA / local-first field workflows
9. design systems / visual storytelling / interaction trends
10. security / privacy / auth patterns for AI-assisted apps

## Source priority

Prefer:
1. primary product documentation / release notes;
2. original research reports with methodology;
3. respected technical/product publications;
4. customer/community evidence when useful for lived experience.

Avoid building roadmap from:
- unsourced social hype;
- SEO listicles;
- one influencer opinion;
- one vendor marketing claim without corroboration.

Use current dated sources. Distinguish source fact from inference.

## Radar process

For every meaningful signal:

### SIGNAL
What changed?

### SOURCE
Who says so? Date? Primary or secondary?

### EVIDENCE QUALITY
HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

### WHY IT MATTERS TO DUTRA
Which real seller/customer problem could it improve?

### CURRENT DUTRA CAPABILITY
What already exists that overlaps?

### GAP
What is genuinely missing?

### CLASSIFICATION
- ADOPT
- TEST
- WATCH
- DEFER
- REJECT

### SMALLEST EXPERIMENT
What can be tested cheaply before architecture/code commitment?

### RISK
Data, complexity, vendor lock-in, security, UX, cost, maintenance.

### OWNER / SOURCE OF TRUTH
Which existing DUTRA owner must remain canonical?

### BRAIN ACTION
None / Idea / Pattern / Decision / Open Question / Incident.

## Trend-to-product rule

A market trend is never automatically a feature.

Run:
SIGNAL → evidence → current problem → gap → experiment → evidence → decision.

Do not duplicate engines because a competitor has a feature.

## Design / proposal benchmark rule

For visual/reference products such as Gamma, Figma, Qwilr, PandaDoc, etc.:
- extract interaction/content patterns;
- separate pattern reuse from tool integration;
- prefer modularity, editability, theming and fast comprehension;
- keep DUTRA business truth inside canonical owners.

## AI / sales benchmark rule

Track especially:
- seller time saved;
- research automation;
- quote/proposal acceleration;
- account understanding;
- next-best-action;
- human + agent handoff;
- unified/clean data requirements.

Do not equate AI adoption with value. Require workflow evidence.
